About the analysis

Linear-elastic finite elements (3-node triangles) with patch-recovered nodal stresses. Units: mm, N, MPa. Stresses at sharp inside corners and at the ends of fixed edges are theoretical singularities: they keep rising as the mesh is refined, so judge the part by stresses away from those points or add a fillet.
